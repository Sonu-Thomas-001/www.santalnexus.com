Trade Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information for international produce buyers and trade partners.

What is the typical transit duration from your port to Southeast Asia?

Ocean freight transit from VOC Port (Tuticorin) or Chennai Port to Port Klang (Malaysia) is approximately 5 to 7 days, and to Singapore is 4 to 6 days. For express consignments, air cargo from Madurai (IXM) or Chennai (MAA) reaches within 24 to 36 hours.

What are your accepted international payment terms?

We work with standard international instruments: Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC at sight from prime international banks) or advance Telegraphic Transfer (T/T deposit balance against draft Bill of Lading and shipping documents). For verified repeat partners, credit terms can be structured.

What export documents do you provide with each shipment?

Every commercial consignment includes: Original Commercial Invoice, Detailed Packing List, Clean On-Board Ocean Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB), Government Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin (COO), and Temperature Logger Trip Report. Independent SGS inspection reports are provided upon request.

Can you customize carton packaging and branding?

Yes. For contractual container programs, we can print buyer-specific artwork, brand logos, barcodes, PLU stickers, net weights, and regulatory language directly on the export cartons and inner liners.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for international orders?

For ocean shipments, our typical MOQ is one 20ft Reefer container (~10 to 12 MT) or one 40ft High Cube container (~20 to 24 MT). For air shipments of high-value fresh vegetables and greens, the MOQ starts at 500 kg to 1 MT.
